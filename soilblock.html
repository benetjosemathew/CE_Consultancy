6A. Visual Inspection & Dimensions (IS 1725:2023 Cl. 5.2, 7, Annex B)

Visual (Cl. 5.2): blocks sound, free from cracks and nodules of free lime; faces flat and rectangular, opposite faces parallel, angles square. Minor chipping from handling during delivery is not a reason for rejection, so do not count it. Dimensions (Annex B): steel scale or tape in 1 mm divisions. Length on the longitudinal centre line of each face, width across the top and bottom bearing surfaces at mid-length, height on all four vertical faces at mid-length. 6B. Dry Density (IS 1725:2023 Cl. 8.1, Annex C)

Dry the blocks at 100 ± 5 °C to constant mass (two weighings 2 h apart differ by not more than 0.05 %). Measure the dried block to the nearest 1 mm and weigh to the nearest 2 g. Dry density = mass × 10⁹ / volume (kg/m³). Average of 3 blocks not less than 1750 kg/m³, reported to the nearest 1 kg/m³.

6C. Wet Compressive Strength (IS 1725:2023 Cl. 8.2; IS 3495 (Part 1) : 2019)

Blocks immersed in water at 27 ± 2 °C for 24 h, surplus water drained, frog and any voids in the bed faces filled with cement-sand mortar, then tested between 3 mm plywood sheets at 7 ± 1 N/mm²/min (IS 3495 (Part 1), 4.1). Strength = max load / bed area. Average reported to 0.1 N/mm². No individual block may be more than 15 % below the class value (Cl. 8.2.1.1). If one block fails this, ignore it, test two more, and the set of four must comply (Cl. 8.2.1.2); add the two rows and tick "Ignored" on the failed one.

6E. Linear Expansion on Saturation (IS 1725:2023 Cl. 8.4, Annex D)

Cure 28 days (IS 17165, 6.8), oven dry at 60 ± 5 °C to constant mass and cool. Measure the longest dimension with a vernier (0.1 mm) as the initial length. Take dial gauge readings at the metal strip, then turn the block 90° and read again; repeat after 24 h soaking. Expansion = mean final reading − mean initial reading. Percentage = expansion / initial length × 100. Average of 3 blocks not more than 0.10 %, reported to 0.01 %.

6F. Weathering (IS 1725:2023 Cl. 8.5, Annex E)

Dry at 60 ± 5 °C to constant mass (initial mass). Each cycle: soak 5 h, dry at 70 ± 5 °C for 42 h, brush all six faces twice with the wire brush at about 15 N. After 12 cycles dry at 60 ± 5 °C to constant mass (final mass). Mass loss = (initial − final) / initial × 100. Average of 3 blocks not more than 3 %, reported to 0.1 %.
